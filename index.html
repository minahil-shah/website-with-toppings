Revolution of Money: An Invention That Went Through Many Phases

Introduction:

As time passed, the population of people grew, and with the population, the needs of people grew as well. These were the needs required in daily life. So, to fulfill these needs, they used to exchange their items with other people who had the things they needed. Sometimes there was such an exchange in which a small amount was to be exchanged in order to acquire a high amount of the thing needed. This was the case which led to inequality and quarrels. So, to sort this out, things like stones and special shells were used to exchange items. The finer or more valuable the item looked, the more stones or shells were added in terms of exchange. However, people were also not satisfied with this matter and decided to invent such a valuable thing that could be counted easily in order to serve equality and get the things they desired. So, coins were made out of silver and gold in order to serve this purpose. Wherever people used to go, they carried these coins in a bag. Therefore, these coins were very heavy to carry, so to solve this problem, people invented money, which was made from cotton and linen. This currency, because of its multiple benefits, was then used all over the world. After this invention, a lot more inventions came into sight, such as credit cards and digital currency. In the future, there are expected to be more variations in our means of trade.

Not only this, but it has also made the process of transferring money to bank accounts or paying bills easier and quicker. The revolution of money itself is a very great thing that has brought many changes across the world. From safe investment to the easier use of money without taking the tension of robbery, the revolution of money has made everything feel safer and more convenient.
